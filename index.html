How we rank

Every firm on this site is evaluated on the same six criteria — licensure and coverage, discipline breadth, turnaround and responsiveness, project-type fit for Seattle buyers, capacity model, and buyer proof — from public-record desk research, in the same order, whether the firm is a flagship or a specialist. Seattle's defining condition is seismic: structural seriousness is not a differentiator here, it is the entry fee — so this ranking separates firms on breadth, capacity, and speed once that bar is cleared.

Why coverage decides this market

Seattle buyers build across the whole Pacific Northwest. A tech campus program here extends to the Eastside and beyond state lines; a multifamily developer works Tacoma, Bellevue, and Portland from one desk; a port-industrial operator's next facility sits in another jurisdiction entirely. The criterion most rankings ignore — licensure and coverage — is the one those buyers feel first, and it is where this ranking is won: the firm at the top can take the Seattle job and the next three states' worth of jobs behind it, sealing in-house under responsible charge instead of handing the work to a stranger.
